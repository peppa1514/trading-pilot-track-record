What is the lab?

20 ways of trading the same 8 cryptos, each with its own simulated $1,000, all started on 15 Oct 2026. Nothing was tuned after the start; the rules were published before day 1.

Same real prices, fees and funding as the live account. The money is pretend. Not investment advice.

Under 3 months of data, the ranking is mostly luck.

Under 6 months, treat it as a hint.
