Results under ~6 months are mostly noise. Twenty strategies, each with $1,000 of simulated money, run in parallel on the same 8 Binance Futures markets with real prices, fees (0.05%) and funding. Every strategy was pre-registered with frozen code and parameters (SHA-256) before its forward test started; parameters are taken from the published sources, not optimized. Not investment advice.

Historical backtests (not live)

These are backtests, not live results: the same simulator run on 2023-12 → the day before registration. They prove nothing (the rules were known to have worked or not when they were chosen) and they are excluded from the ranking. t-stat = mean daily return / its standard error.
